## Long description

Hemen Pergola is the marketplace that connects customers with verified pergola and
outdoor-structure manufacturers.

**As a customer:** Describe your project once — dimensions, product, location. See instant
price bands from verified manufacturers covering your area, and send an offer request to
up to five of them in one step. Compare the formal offers that arrive (VAT included, line
by line) next to the original estimate; track the site survey and the whole process in the
app. When the work is done, rate your experience — reviews are moderated before
publication.

**As a manufacturer:** New leads reach your pocket; you answer the 48-hour response window
from the app. Accept a lead, schedule the survey, send the offer from your phone, mark the
outcome. Customer contact details are shared only after you accept — and only with the
customer's consent.

**Principles:** Contact details are never shared before acceptance. The price band is an
estimate and excludes VAT; the formal offer includes it. You stay in control of your data:
manage notification preferences in the app, download or erase your data from your web
account.
